## Summary

Ph.D. student in Computer Application Technology at **Dalian University of Technology**. My research focuses on computer vision, complex-scene perception, video instance segmentation, human pose estimation, RGB-Event multimodal perception, and spatiotemporal modeling. I have experience with Transformer-based visual representations, model training and optimization, dataset construction, experimental evaluation, and inference-efficiency analysis. I have participated in the Huawei Innovation Research Program, the Science and Technology Innovation 2030 major project, and defense-related projects.

## Research and Project Experience

- **RGB-Event Multimodal Video Instance Segmentation**<br>
  *Video understanding and cross-modal fusion*
  - Study instance-level temporal perception by fusing RGB and event information, with a focus on adapting RGB-pretrained instance segmentation models to the event modality
  - Responsible for model adaptation, event-information integration, experimental validation, and result analysis
  - First-author manuscript submitted to AAAI 2027

- **Adaptive Vision Transformer for Human Pose Estimation**<br>
  *Structured visual understanding and efficient inference*
  - Designed an adaptive Vision Transformer to focus computation on informative regions in spatially sparse event data
  - Responsible for model architecture, training and optimization, ablation studies, and efficiency evaluation
  - Participated in constructing EventMM-HPE, a 240 Hz multi-person, multi-action human pose dataset
  - Achieved 179 FPS inference; first-author paper published at ACM Multimedia 2024

- **Huawei Innovation Research Program: Event-Camera Visual Algorithms**<br>
  *Oct 2021 - Oct 2023*
  - Studied event-image semantic segmentation and gait recognition for low-light, high-dynamic-range, and fast-motion scenarios
  - Built the Carla-Semantic dataset; improved semantic segmentation by 6.51% mIoU and 4.93% MPA
  - Responsible for event-stream processing, event representations, model implementation, and experimental evaluation
  - Produced journal publications and patent outcomes

- **DeeCamp 2019: AI-Generated Poster System**<br>
  *Jul 2019 - Sep 2019*
  - Helped design and develop an end-to-end poster-generation prototype spanning image processing, text keyword extraction, and poster composition
  - Collected and processed foreground images; compared R3Net and used PoolNet for vehicle foreground segmentation
  - Used BERT for keyword extraction, perceptual-loss-based style transfer, and SRGAN for super-resolution
  - [Project demonstration](https://b23.tv/hPeDcxa)

- **Science and Technology Innovation 2030 Major Project**<br>
  *Complex Dynamic Systems: Intelligent Theory and Methods, Jan 2022 - Dec 2024*
  - Studied asynchronous event processing, multi-frequency hypergraph fusion, and multi-scale spatiotemporal relation modeling
  - Responsible for spatiotemporal modeling, hypergraph perception algorithms, implementation, experimental validation, and result analysis
  - Produced journal publications and one patent application or grant
