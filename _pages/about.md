## Research Focus

<div class="home-grid">
  <section class="home-card">
    <h3>Video Understanding and Segmentation</h3>
    <p>Video instance segmentation, semantic segmentation, and spatiotemporal relation modeling for complex and dynamic visual scenes.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="home-card">
    <h3>RGB-Event Multimodal Perception</h3>
    <p>Cross-modal fusion and adaptation of RGB-pretrained models to event data for low-light, high-dynamic-range, and fast-motion scenarios.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="home-card">
    <h3>Efficient Human-Centric Vision</h3>
    <p>Adaptive Transformer representations for sparse event data, with applications in high-frequency human pose estimation and efficient inference.</p>
  </section>
</div>

## 研究方向

<div class="home-grid">
  <section class="home-card">
    <h3>视频理解与目标分割</h3>
    <p>面向复杂动态场景的视频实例分割、语义分割及时空关系建模。</p>
  </section>
  <section class="home-card">
    <h3>RGB-Event 多模态感知</h3>
    <p>研究 RGB 与事件模态的跨模态融合，以及 RGB 预训练模型在事件数据上的适配，用于低照度、高动态范围和快速运动场景。</p>
  </section>
  <section class="home-card">
    <h3>高效人体视觉理解</h3>
    <p>面向稀疏事件数据的自适应 Transformer 表征，并应用于高频人体姿态估计与高效推理。</p>
  </section>
</div>
