I am a Ph.D. student at **Dalian University of Technology**.  
My research interests lie in the field of **computer vision**, with a particular focus on:

- Event-based vision  
- Instance segmentation  
- Efficient deep learning models  

My current research explores learning from event-driven data for visual perception tasks, aiming to design adaptive and efficient architectures for high-speed and high-dynamic-range vision applications.
