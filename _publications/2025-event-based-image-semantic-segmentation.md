## Overview

This work addresses semantic segmentation under challenging illumination and high-speed motion using event cameras. It introduces **EVISS**, which combines an improved graph-Laplacian formulation with attention-based modeling to strengthen global connectivity and contextual dependencies in sparse event data.

## Dataset and Results

The study constructs **Carla-Semantic**, a large-scale simulated event-camera semantic-segmentation dataset with high-precision annotations. On Carla-Semantic, EVISS reports **87.89% MPA** and **81.93% mIoU**.

## 工作概述

本研究面向动态光照和高速运动条件下的图像语义分割问题，引入事件相机并提出 **EVISS**。该方法结合改进的图拉普拉斯公式与注意力机制，增强稀疏事件数据的全局联系和上下文依赖建模能力。

## 数据集与结果

研究构建了高精度标注的大规模仿真事件相机语义分割数据集 **Carla-Semantic**。EVISS 在该数据集上报告 **87.89% MPA** 和 **81.93% mIoU**。
