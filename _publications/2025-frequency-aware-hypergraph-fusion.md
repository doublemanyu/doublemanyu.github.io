## Overview

This work proposes a multi-frequency hypergraph fusion method for event-based semantic segmentation. A frequency-separation module decomposes event frames into multi-scale spatiotemporal features, while dynamic hypergraph construction and hypergraph convolution model long-range dependencies across frequency components.

## Results

The method is evaluated on **Carla-Semantic** and **DDD17-Semantic**. The reported results reach **88.21% MPA** and **82.68% mIoU**, supporting the effectiveness of multi-frequency hypergraph modeling for event-based scene understanding.

## 工作概述

本研究提出面向事件相机语义分割的多频超图融合方法。该方法通过频率分离模块将事件帧分解为多尺度时空特征，并利用动态超图构建和超图卷积建模不同频率成分之间的长程依赖。

## 实验结果

方法在 **Carla-Semantic** 和 **DDD17-Semantic** 数据集上进行验证，报告结果达到 **88.21% MPA** 和 **82.68% mIoU**，表明多频超图建模能够提升事件视觉场景理解能力。
